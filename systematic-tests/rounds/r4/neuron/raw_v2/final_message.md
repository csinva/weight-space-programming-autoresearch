How I set the numbers:
- **Size:** each edit's down_proj column norm compared with its layer's median and max, and the gate_proj norm as a rough sign of how strongly the neuron fires.
- **Layer:** "multiply by 5" edits add 4× the neuron's output, so late-layer ones (layers 22, 23, 18) get the largest predictions. Early-layer and "zero the neuron" edits are kept near 0, as the README's calibration note suggests.
- **Prompt:** the same base size is scaled per prompt. It is smallest for P6 (Shakespeare, p=0.994) and largest for P7 ("are", p=0.27) and P4 (Paris, p=0.47), the least confident targets.
- **Sign:** every dlogp is negative. That is an assumption that disrupting a confident prediction usually hurts it, not something I derived from the weights.
- **p_flip:** I only expect flips on P7, and only for the larger late edits.

So the ranking across edits rests on norms and layer depth, and the ranking across prompts is a fixed pattern applied to every edit.
