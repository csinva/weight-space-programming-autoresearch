I read all 12 weight files and estimated each effect from the probability tables in README.md. I couldn't run any code, so the large drops below are reasoned estimates, not measurements.

How I estimated:
- **Swaps:** the edited tokens never appear as inputs in the affected prompts, except ' and' in P5 and P6. So most swaps just exchange two tokens' scores without moving the total. The target ends up with its partner's old probability. Every other prompt's dlogp is 0.
- **Copies:** the target ends up with the same score as the source token. Its probability is the source's probability, rescaled after the target's original share is removed.
- **Ties:** where the two copies tie exactly, I assumed the model picks the lower token id. That decides whether TO05/P2 and TO10/P1 count as a flip.
- **Zeroed rows and unrelated swaps:** the target's score drops to 0 or near the bottom. I estimated dlogp at about −9 to −14 nats. These numbers are the least certain.
