The predictions rest on three facts about tied embeddings:
1. **Zeroing a token's row** sets its output logit to exactly 0. If that token is a prompt's target, it collapses (about −12 to −17 nats).

3. **A swap or copy moves a logit to another token.** The target takes over the other token's logit at that position, and vice versa.
