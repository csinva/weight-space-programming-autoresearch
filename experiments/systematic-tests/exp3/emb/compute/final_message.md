**What I did.** I read all 24 weight files. Each edit changes one token's logit by c·(1+w_d)·n_d, and the gain (1+w_d) is large in every file (3.36 to 6.03). For each prompt I computed s, the probability-weighted z-score of the listed tokens' column entries, and used n_d ≈ 0.6·s. I then renormalised over the listed top-50 tokens. For tokens not in the top-50 lists I assumed log-probabilities of about −10 to −20, depending on the context.

**Where the uncertainty is:**
- **Size of n_d.** s gives a reasonable sign, but the 0.6·s scaling is my own choice.
