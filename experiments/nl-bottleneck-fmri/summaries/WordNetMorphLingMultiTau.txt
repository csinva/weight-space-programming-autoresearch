Untrained, with no neural network, the model builds about 1,800 hand-made lexical features. Per word, it uses WordNet through NLTK: a normalized histogram of its synsets over the 45 lexnames, a second histogram that also counts synonyms, hypernyms and definition words, and synset rarity and depth. It adds membership in 72 curated semantic categories (contents unknown), 50 function-word indicators, 14 suffix/prefix flags, 19 closed-class sets such as pronouns by person, wh-words, modals and negation, crude syllable and letter-class phonology, and small lists of mental verbs, deixis words and discourse markers. Within each 10-gram, features are summed almost uniformly and the last word is kept separately; tense-cue rates, question and exclamation marks, and part-of-speech and supersense entropy are added. Across the story, the newest word's features feed exponential running averages with time constants of 5, 20, 100 and 500 words, plus short-minus-long differences, distance and cosine surprise against those averages, log steps since recent sentence ends or pronouns, novelty rate and story position. Suffix rules approximate tense and part of speech. Dimensions with variance under 0.0022 on the first story are dropped, leaving about 360. Test correlation was about 0.065.
