No training, no neural network. The model builds handcrafted per-word features dominated by position in the story and sentence; position alone does most of the work.

Position features, each scaled to variance 0.5: for story position p = index/length, Fourier sin/cos k=1–10, Chebyshev and Legendre polynomials to degree 10, about 65 Gaussian bumps of several widths, and log distance from start and end. Sentences end at a final . ? or !; "paragraphs" merge sentences into units of 10–320+ words. Each unit adds relative position, log edge distances, log length, first/last flags and low Fourier terms. Exponential decays since the last and until the next sentence end, and separately for each of . ? !, use time constants of 2–200 words.

Content uses NLTK WordNet (45 lexnames, direct and expanded via hypernyms and glosses), an unseen external table of 72 semantic categories, word lists (pronouns, modals, negation), suffix rules and letter-based phonology. These are summed over the 10-gram and tracked as running averages at τ=5–500. Novelty features: first occurrence, log gap since last use, type-token ratios. Dimensions with variance under 0.05 on the first story are dropped. Raw counts leak story length.
