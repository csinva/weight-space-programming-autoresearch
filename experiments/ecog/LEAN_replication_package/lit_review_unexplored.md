# Lit review: unexplored closed-form features for speech -> ECoG high-gamma (patient1)

Generated by the deep-research workflow (111 agents, verified against the 170 melspec snapshots).

## Summary

Four closed-form/no-training feature families are well-supported by auditory-cortex evidence and NOT yet in the team feature search (verified by grep across all 170 run-library snapshots in runs-ecog/ecog-speech-23608983/interpretable_transformers_lib/). (1) SPEAKER-NORMALIZED RELATIVE features: per-speaker F0 z-score / running-baseline-normalized pitch (Tang/Chang Science 2017: 84pct of intonation electrodes better predicted by relative than absolute pitch; r_rel 0.85 vs r_abs 0.66) and context/running-mean-normalized formants esp F1 (Sjerps/Chang NatComm 2019: STG vowel responses shift contrastively with preceding-speaker F1 range). Biggest genuinely-untried gap. (2) A TRUE peakRate landmark: a sparse magnitude-weighted impulse train at local maxima of the FIRST DERIVATIVE of a broadband loudness envelope (Oganian/Chang ScienceAdvances 2019; Hamilton/Chang Cell 2021) - the discrete event-train beats the continuous envelope for middle-STG high-gamma; team has only continuous per-band ReLU(band minus running-mean) onset proxies. (3) POWER-LAW (scale-free, no fixed time constant) adaptation kernels (Zilany/Carney 2010; Drew/Abbott 2006) on mel/band energy, distinct from the fixed-tau leaky-mean adaptation already tried. (4) A SILENCE-GATED sentence-onset detector (posterior-STG onset zone, Hamilton 2018/2021). A Glasberg-Moore/TVL loudness pipeline is a weaker fifth (EMEG not ECoG; loudness-beats-envelope refuted 0-3). Peripheral AN/IHC models (Zilany-Bruce, Meddis/Sumner, Nelson-Carney SFIE) are valid fixed front-ends in principle but BLOCKED here: the embedder reads ONLY the 30Hz mel-spectrogram (src/data.py), not the raw waveform they require; only their sub-30Hz dynamics port over, and team filterbank attempts already underperformed.

## Findings (prioritized)

### 1. HIGHEST PRIORITY (untried, computable from 30Hz melspec): speaker-normalized RELATIVE PITCH (per-speaker F0 z-score, or F0 minus/divided-by a running per-speaker baseline) is what STG high-gamma encodes for intonation, NOT absolute pitch. Normalize the harmonic-comb F0 estimate already in the pipeline by a slow leaky baseline; add its temporal derivative (pitch-change).
*confidence: high | verification vote: 3-0 (claims 2,3,10)*

Tang/Hamilton/Chang Science 2017 (10.1126/science.aam8577): 84pct of intonation electrodes (38/45) better predicted by relative pitch; relative-pitch encoding predicts intonation-contour discriminability r=0.57 (P<1e-16) vs absolute r=0.03 (P=0.67); cross-speaker similarity r_rel 0.85 vs r_abs 0.66. Relative pitch is a per-speaker z-score of ln(Hz). Missing-fundamental stimuli decoded 96pct as well as normal speech (periodicity/pitch-salience, not F0-band energy). Replicated Li NatComm 2021, Chen INTERSPEECH 2025. VERIFIED: grep of all 170 snapshots found NO relative-pitch/speaker-norm/F0-z-score file; team has only ABSOLUTE pitch salience (HarmonicComb_8F0_b48, MelRmRelu90Harmonic_b48 ~0.052). The comb already gives a per-frame F0 from the 30Hz mel grid; subtract/divide the existing causal running-mean primitive (MelMinusRunningMean, block 48) for closed-form relative pitch. Turns unsegmented, so a slow leaky baseline approximates the per-speaker mean.

Sources: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aam8577; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9447996/

### 2. HIGH PRIORITY (untried, computable): context/speaker-normalized FORMANTS, especially F1 minus a running-mean F1 (contrast enhancement). STG/parabelt vowel responses are contrastive: an identical target F1 evokes a higher-F1-category response after a low-F1 context. Add formant (or spectral-peak) values MINUS a slow running mean.
*confidence: high | verification vote: 3-0 (claims 6,7)*

Sjerps/Fox/Johnson/Chang NatComm 2019 (PMC6549175), 256-electrode ECoG, high-gamma 70-150Hz, parabelt/STG: vowel representation shifts contrastively relative to the preceding speaker; responsiveness modulated by the preceding F1 range. F1 is the primary vowel cue; the operation is running-mean subtraction (no learning). CONTRAST: run library has many ABSOLUTE formant features (FormantF1F2F3_b48, FormantF4_b48, FormantTraj_b48, FormValley_b48, FormLocalProm_b48; top scorers ~0.0617-0.0619) but NONE normalized to a running context/speaker mean. Formant-minus-slow-running-mean is the untried contrastive extension, buildable with the existing running-mean primitive. Caveat: F1 on conversational speech is noisy, so pair raw F1 with leaky-normalized F1.

Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6549175/

### 3. HIGH PRIORITY (untried, computable): a TRUE peakRate landmark = a sparse magnitude-weighted impulse train at local maxima of the first time-derivative of a broadband loudness envelope (optionally with a small post-peak kernel). Best-established acoustic driver of middle-STG high-gamma, distinct from the team continuous per-band onset proxies.
*confidence: high | verification vote: 3-0 (claims 0,1,4,9)*

Oganian/Chang ScienceAdvances 2019 (10.1126/sciadv.aay6279): middle STG detects local maxima in envelope rate of change; the DISCRETE peakRate event-train model OUTPERFORMS the continuous-envelope model (R2 mean 0.22 vs 0.17 on 331 STG electrodes); responses scale with peakRate magnitude (steeper slopes, greater responses; amplitude change not absolute). peakRate (rate-of-change) not peakEnv drives responses. Hamilton/Chang Cell 2021 (PMC8456481) maps it to middle STG. Released code (github yoganian/peakRate, find_peakRate.m) is closed-form: envelope then diff then findpeaks. COMPUTABILITY: broadband loudness = sum/mean over mel bins (team has log_E_pos); first difference then local-maxima then magnitude-weighted sparse feature. CONTRAST: grep found NO peakRate/findpeaks/local-max-of-derivative; closest are continuous per-band ReLU(band minus running-mean) (Phon8_BandMemW15, BinMemW15) and flux (MelRmFlux, BinFlux3). Discreteness plus magnitude-weighting is what made the event model win.

Sources: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aay6279; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8456481/

### 4. MEDIUM-HIGH PRIORITY (untried, computable): a SILENCE-GATED sentence/utterance-onset detector firing only on acoustic onsets after roughly 200ms of low energy. Posterior/lateral STG has a localized onset-selective population (strong transient high-gamma at sentence onset then quiescence), separable from sustained sites.
*confidence: high | verification vote: 3-0 (claims 5,8)*

Hamilton/Edwards/Chang Current Biology 2018 (PMID 29861132, high-density ECoG, 27 participants) plus Cell 2021 (PMC8456481): caudal/posterior STG shows strong transient responses at sentence onset then quiescence, distinct from rostral sustained; onset responses decode sentence/phrase boundaries. Review PMC9447996: posterior STG responds to onset following at least 200ms of silence; also responds to non-speech onsets (acoustic edges, closed-form). COMPUTABILITY: broadband loudness on the 30Hz grid; fire when energy rises above threshold AND the preceding ~6 frames (200ms) are below a silence threshold. CONTRAST: team has generic ReLU rising-edge onsets but NONE silence-gated. Lower priority than peakRate because the zone is spatially localized (helps specific posterior electrodes, not all 166).

Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8456481/; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9447996/

### 5. MEDIUM PRIORITY (untried, computable): POWER-LAW adaptation kernels (scale-free, NO fixed time constant) applied to mel/band energy, distinct from the team fixed-tau leaky-mean adaptation. Power-law adaptation produces dynamic-range/level normalization within roughly 1s.
*confidence: high | verification vote: 3-0 (claims 12,13,14,15)*

Zilany/Carney JNeurosci 2010 (PMC2935089): power-law dynamics explained adaptation to sound-level statistics; with only exponential adaptation, NO dynamic-range adaptation; DRA within roughly 1s. Drew/Abbott 2006 (PMID 16641386): power-law has no fixed time constant, intermediate between perfect integration and exponential; Lundstrom NatNeuro 2008 cortical fractional differentiation. COMPUTABILITY: convolve each mel/band channel with a discretized power-law memory kernel proportional to (t plus t0) raised to minus beta (a fixed FIR with power-law-decaying taps), then subtract/divide; closed-form. CONTRAST: run-library adaptation is exclusively fixed-tau leaky/box means (block 16/32/40/48/64; W 4/8/15/30/60). A grep on power was a FALSE POSITIVE (only squaring/cubing for variance/skew moments). A single scale-free power-law kernel is absent and is the principled route to the ~1s level normalization STG exhibits. Caveat: ~1s DRA is an auditory-nerve-model property; STG link via documented context normalization, so a hypothesis.

Sources: https://www.jneurosci.org/content/30/31/10380; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2787068/

### 6. MEDIUM PRIORITY (untried, approximately computable): a psychoacoustic LOUDNESS pipeline (Glasberg-Moore/TVL): ERB-scaled excitation, per-channel compressive nonlinearity, specific loudness in sones, then short-term loudness via a fixed attack/release AGC. A parametric alternative to raw mel plus log/cube-root.
*confidence: medium | verification vote: 3-0 (claims 21,22,23) for definition+EMEG entrainment; sibling loudness-beats-envelope claim REFUTED 0-3; evidence is EMEG not ECoG*

Thwaites 2016 (PMC4848315): instantaneous loudness (Glasberg-Moore) entrains Heschl/dorsal-lateral-sulcus at 45/100/165ms; short-term loudness (AGC with attack/release) at 275ms. Moore TrendsHearing 2016 (PMC5318944): closed-form TVL with ERB excitation, compression, specific loudness per 1-Cam, calibration C=0.063, no training. CONTRAST: grep found NO Glasberg-Moore/sones/specific-loudness/excitation-pattern snapshot; team uses raw mel (dB) and an energy-ratio AGC (AGC_BroadbandLoudness_b48) but NOT ERB excitation-spread, the cochlear compressive nonlinearity to sones, or an asymmetric attack/release AGC. CAVEATS: the full six-FFT TVL (2-64ms windows) needs the waveform and cannot run on the 30Hz grid (only a mel-bins-as-excitation approximation is computable); and loudness-beats-envelope was REFUTED 0-3, so modest gains expected. Value is the compressive/excitation/AGC shaping.

Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4848315/; https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2331216516682698

### 7. STRONGLY SUPPORTED IN PRINCIPLE BUT BLOCKED by the 30Hz melspec input (LOW PRIORITY here): peripheral AN/IHC-synapse models as fixed front-ends (Zilany-Bruce 2009/2014 exponential plus dual power-law AN adaptation; Meddis/Sumner 2002 DRNL then IHC ODE then transmitter-depletion synapse then AN spikes; Nelson-Carney 2004 SFIE band-pass AM, BMFs mostly below 100Hz).
*confidence: high | verification vote: 3-0 (claims 11,16,17,18,19,20); valid no-training models, but computability here is the limitation, verified*

Legitimate physiology-parametric no-training front-ends, exactly the candidate class named. Zilany-Bruce 2009 (PMC2787068), Zilany/Carney 2010: pressure to AN firing rate with exponential plus power-law adaptation; 2020 PNAS Simple transformations capture auditory input to cortex (PMID 33097665) shows AN-fiber periphery models improve LINEAR encoding of auditory cortex. Sumner 2002 (JASA) and Nelson/Carney 2004 SFIE (eNeuro 2015 PMC4596011: no machine learning or parameter optimization). HARD BARRIER (verified src/data.py + wav_files listing): the embedder receives ONLY the mel-spectrogram (50 by T) at 30Hz dB; load_stim loads the melspec npy and nothing else. These models operate on the time-domain PRESSURE WAVEFORM (need 10-20kHz for basilar-membrane/IHC/phase-locking dynamics); they cannot be computed from a 30Hz log-mel grid inside the embedder, and external precompute is forbidden. So NOT directly actionable here. ALTERNATIVE: port only sub-30Hz downstream dynamics that survive: the power-law kernel (other finding) and an SFIE-style band-pass AM via difference-of-leaky-means (team has a related ModBank ~0.0405 but not explicit short-excitation/long-inhibition timing with a nonzero inhibitory delay). Note StrfGabor_b48 (0.0365) and ModBank both UNDERperformed, so modulation-filterbank variants are weaker bets than relative-pitch/peakRate/power-law.

Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2787068/; https://audiolab.usal.es/Documentos/ArticulosLAB/Sumner_etal_2002.pdf; https://www.urmc.rochester.edu/MediaLibraries/URMCMedia/labs/carney-lab/documents/articles/NelsonCarney-JASA-2004.pdf
